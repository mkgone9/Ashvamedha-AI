Common Questions Answered

Q.What financial calculators are available on Ashvamedha Finance website?
We offer 5 free calculators: SIP Calculator, Step-Up SIP Calculator, Lumpsum Investment Calculator, Retirement Corpus Calculator, and Home Buying EMI Calculator.
Q.How accurate is the SIP calculator?
Our SIP calculator uses the exact compound interest formula used by Zerodha and Groww. For example, ₹5,000/month at 12% for 10 years = ₹11,50,193 — verified accurate.
Q.How much SIP do I need for retirement?
Use our free Retirement Calculator. As a benchmark: to retire at 60 with current expenses of ₹50,000/month, you typically need ₹5–8 crore corpus. A ₹15,000–20,000/month SIP started at 30 can achieve this at 12% returns.
