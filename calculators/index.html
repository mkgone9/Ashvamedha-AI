Common Questions Answered

Q.What financial calculators are available on Ashvamedha Finance website?
We offer 11 free calculators: SIP (with SWP), Step-Up SIP, Lumpsum, Retirement Corpus, FIRE Number, Goal Planner, Home Loan vs Investing, Car Loan vs Cash, Rent vs Buy, Mutual Funds vs Real Estate, and FD vs Mutual Fund. Every calculator can be downloaded as a PDF report.
Q.How accurate is the SIP calculator?
Our SIP calculator uses the standard SIP formula used by fund houses and investing apps (monthly rate = annual return ÷ 12, invested at the start of each month). For example, ₹5,000/month at 12% p.a. for 10 years grows to about ₹11.62 lakh. Actual mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.
Q.How much SIP do I need for retirement?
Use our free Retirement Calculator. As a benchmark: to retire at 60 with current expenses of ₹50,000/month, you need roughly ₹5.5–7.5 crore, depending on whether you plan for money to last to age 80 or 90 (6% inflation, 8% return after retirement). Starting at 30 with nothing saved, that takes a SIP of about ₹15,500–21,000/month at 12% returns.
