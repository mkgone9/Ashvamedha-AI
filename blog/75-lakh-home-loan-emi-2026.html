Don't confuse eligibility with affordability

A lender may assess the loan as serviceable, but your household may have other priorities: emergency savings, insurance, children, parents, retirement and investing. The correct amount is personal to your cash flow.

Stress-test the loan

  • Higher interest rate
  • Temporary income interruption
  • Unexpected property expenses
  • Reduced bonus or variable income
Useful test: if a rate increase would force you to stop essential investing or use emergency savings every month, reconsider the loan size.
