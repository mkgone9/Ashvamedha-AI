Home Loan Eligibility Calculator 2026 — Estimate Your Borrowing Capacity

An eligibility calculator is useful for planning, but it is not a promise of approval. Lenders assess documented income, existing obligations, credit information, age, tenure and property details.

Start with your monthly cash flow

  • Monthly take-home or documented income
  • Existing loan EMIs
  • Household living costs
  • Insurance and recurring commitments
  • Expected down payment
  • Preferred tenure
Simple planning approach: calculate an EMI you can live with first, then see what loan amount that EMI supports. This reverses the common mistake of starting with the maximum amount a lender may offer.

Why the same salary gives different results

Suppose two applicants earn the same amount. One has no existing debt; the other has a car loan and personal loan. The second applicant has less free cash flow available for a housing EMI. Tenure and age can also change the assessment.

What the calculator cannot know

A basic online calculator may not know your lender's internal policy, exact credit profile, property valuation, legal status, income documentation or applicable LTV conditions. Treat the output as a planning estimate.

Use three scenarios

  1. Comfortable: your preferred EMI.
  2. Stretch: a higher EMI that still leaves a meaningful buffer.
  3. Stress: test what happens if rates rise or income temporarily falls.
