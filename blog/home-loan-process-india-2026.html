Home Loan Process in India 2026 — From Application to Disbursement

A home loan typically involves more than submitting an application. The lender assesses the borrower, documents and property before the final disbursement.

1. Estimate affordability
Set a realistic property budget, down payment and EMI before applying.
2. Compare lenders
Compare rate structure, fees, reset terms, tenure and service—not only advertisements.
3. Submit application
Provide applicant, income, identity and other requested details.
4. Credit and income assessment
The lender evaluates repayment capacity and credit information.
5. Property legal/technical checks
The lender may assess title, approvals, valuation and other property factors.
6. Sanction
The sanction letter states key approved terms and conditions.
7. Agreement and conditions
Review the loan agreement, charges and required conditions carefully.
8. Disbursement
Funds are released according to the lender's process and transaction conditions.

Where delays usually happen

  • Incomplete income documentation
  • Unclear or inconsistent bank transactions
  • Property document issues
  • Pending approvals or legal checks
  • Differences between application information and supporting documents
