Fixed vs Floating Home Loan in 2026 — What Should You Compare?

The central difference is how the interest rate can change. “Fixed” and “floating” can have product-specific definitions, so read the lender's agreement carefully rather than relying on the label alone.

CompareFixed-type structureFloating-type structure
Rate movementMore predictable during the fixed periodCan change under the applicable benchmark/reset structure
EMI certaintyGenerally greater during fixed periodLess certain
Interest-rate riskLower during fixed periodBorrower bears more movement risk

Floating rates are not automatically bad

A floating loan can benefit when applicable rates fall, but it can also become more expensive when rates rise. The important question is whether your cash flow can absorb movement.

Ask the lender: What is the benchmark? What is the spread? How often can the rate reset? How do EMI and tenure change?
