Rent vs Buy in India 2026 — Should You Take a Home Loan?

Rent is an expense, but buying also has costs. A useful comparison includes rent, EMI, down payment, transaction costs, maintenance, property taxes where applicable, expected holding period and the opportunity cost of the money used for the purchase.

Buying gives you

  • Ownership of the property if the loan is repaid
  • Potential protection from future rent increases
  • Potential property appreciation, but no guarantee
  • Long-term housing security

Renting gives you

  • Flexibility to relocate
  • Lower upfront capital requirement
  • Less direct responsibility for some property costs
  • Ability to invest the capital that would otherwise be tied up
The hidden comparison: compare the opportunity cost of the down payment and purchase costs, not just rent versus EMI.

When the decision changes

A person expecting to stay in a city for many years may evaluate buying differently from someone whose career may require relocation in two years. Property price, rent, financing cost and household stability all matter.

Don't assume appreciation

Property values vary by location, property type and cycle. Treat future appreciation as uncertain rather than as a guaranteed return that makes any purchase worthwhile.
