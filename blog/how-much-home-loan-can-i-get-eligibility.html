What This Estimate Is Actually Based On

The calculator above uses a common industry approximation: lenders typically cap your total EMI obligations at roughly 40-50% of net monthly income — commonly called FOIR. This is a genuinely useful starting estimate, but it's a simplification of what real underwriting actually weighs.

What Real Underwriting Adds on Top of Income

  • Credit score — a strong score (typically 750+) can improve both your eligibility and the interest rate offered
  • Age and remaining working years — lenders cap loan tenure so it ends around typical retirement age
  • Employment stability and type — salaried applicants with longer tenure, or self-employed applicants with consistent documentation, are generally viewed more favorably
  • Co-applicant income — adding a co-applicant can meaningfully increase eligibility
  • Existing loans beyond EMI amount — a large outstanding credit card balance can affect the assessment

Why Two People With the Same Income Can Get Different Offers

Two people with identical salaries can receive meaningfully different loan offers based on credit score, existing debt, employer category, and the specific bank's current lending appetite. The calculator gives a reasonable planning estimate — your actual sanctioned amount can differ once full underwriting is complete.
