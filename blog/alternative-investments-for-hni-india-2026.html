Alternative Investments for HNIs in India 2026 — A Simple Guide

Alternative investments are often discussed as if they are automatically better than traditional investments. They are not. Their main difference is that they can provide access to different strategies, private markets or less traditional assets, often with higher complexity and lower liquidity.

Think in terms of role: an alternative investment should have a clear purpose in the portfolio before you consider the product.

Why HNIs consider alternatives

  • Potential diversification beyond listed markets.
  • Access to private businesses or strategies.
  • Different return drivers.
  • Ability to invest with a longer time horizon.

The hidden cost: illiquidity

If a listed investment can usually be sold through a market but a private investment may require a buyer, fund exit or contractual process, the two should not be treated as equivalent. Before investing, ask how and when you can realistically get your money back.

How much should be allocated?

There is no universal percentage that fits every HNI. Start with liquidity needs, existing business and real-estate exposure, upcoming obligations, risk tolerance and the time horizon. A family already heavily exposed to one private business may need more diversification rather than another private investment.

Due diligence checklist

  1. Understand exactly what the vehicle owns.
  2. Check the legal and regulatory structure.
  3. Read fees and all expenses.
  4. Understand valuation methodology.
  5. Check lock-in and exit mechanisms.
  6. Study leverage and downside scenarios.
  7. Review the manager's track record in context.
  8. Understand taxation separately.

One simple rule

Do not buy an alternative investment because it is labelled “HNI”, “exclusive” or “private”. Buy only when you understand its role, risks, costs and liquidity inside your overall wealth plan.

Building an alternative-investment portfolio?

Review the whole balance sheet first. The goal is diversification and fit, not simply adding more complex products.
