Best Broker to Invest in US Stocks From India: What Should You Compare?

The cheapest-looking broker is not always the lowest-cost or most suitable option for an Indian investor.

What to compare

  • Regulatory status and the entity actually holding your account
  • Brokerage, platform and other account charges
  • USD/INR conversion and remittance costs
  • Fractional-share availability
  • How corporate actions and dividends are handled
  • Tax statements and transaction records
  • Customer support and withdrawal process
  • How your US assets are held and what investor protections apply

Broker cost is more than brokerage

For an Indian investor, the total cost can include currency conversion, bank/remittance charges, platform fees and taxes. Compare the complete transaction path rather than a headline “zero brokerage” claim.

Important: Your LRS remittance, TCS treatment and tax reporting are separate from the broker's advertised trading fee.

Questions to ask before opening an account

  1. Which legal entity will hold my account?
  2. Where will my securities and cash be custodied?
  3. What documents will I receive for Indian tax reporting?
  4. How are dividends and withholding handled?
  5. What happens if I stop using the platform?

Do not choose a broker only because it offers popular US stocks

Access to Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia or ETFs is only one part of the decision. Portfolio construction, tax reporting, currency risk and long-term asset allocation matter more once the portfolio becomes meaningful.
