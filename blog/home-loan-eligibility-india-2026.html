Home Loan Eligibility in India 2026 — How Much Home Loan Can I Get?

Home-loan eligibility is not simply a percentage of salary. Lenders generally look at income, existing obligations, credit history, age, repayment capacity, tenure and the property being financed. Your sanctioned amount can therefore differ from an online calculator.

Important: bank eligibility is a maximum lending assessment. Your own affordable borrowing amount may be lower.

What determines eligibility?

  • Income: stable documented income supports repayment capacity.
  • Existing EMIs: car, personal, education and other loans reduce available repayment capacity.
  • Age and tenure: the lender considers the remaining earning/repayment period.
  • Credit profile: repayment history and credit information can affect approval and pricing.
  • Property: legal, technical and valuation checks matter.
  • Down payment: the borrower must fund the required portion not covered by the loan.

Think of eligibility as a funnel

Income starts the assessment, existing obligations narrow it, tenure changes the EMI, and property valuation can further constrain the final loan.

Gross/documented income → obligations → repayment capacity → property/LTV → final sanction.

Why two people with the same salary can get different loans

Applicant A may have no existing debt and a strong documented income. Applicant B may have several EMIs or variable income. Their eligibility can be materially different even when headline salary is identical.

Documents commonly requested

  • Identity and address proof
  • Salary slips and bank statements for salaried applicants
  • ITRs, financial statements and bank statements for many self-employed applicants
  • Property documents required by the lender
  • Existing-loan details where applicable

Don't borrow to the maximum

Before accepting a sanction, calculate the EMI alongside living costs, insurance, emergency reserves, investments and future family goals. A lender may approve an amount that is technically serviceable but uncomfortable for your household.
