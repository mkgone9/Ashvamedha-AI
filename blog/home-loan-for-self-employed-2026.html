Home Loan for Self-Employed in India 2026 — Eligibility, ITR & Documents

Self-employed applicants can obtain home loans, but income documentation may require more detail because cash flows can be variable. Lenders commonly examine tax returns, financial statements, bank statements and business information as applicable.

Prepare the income story

  • ITRs and computation where applicable
  • Financial statements as requested
  • Bank statements
  • Business registration/constitution documents where applicable
  • Existing liabilities

Why turnover is not the same as income

A business can have high turnover but lower taxable or assessed income after expenses. Home-loan assessment therefore cannot be based on revenue alone.

Consistency helps: keep tax filings, financial statements and bank flows reasonably consistent and be ready to explain large changes.

Existing business loans

Business and personal liabilities can affect repayment capacity. Disclose them accurately rather than focusing only on the new home-loan EMI.

Property still matters

Even a strong income profile does not guarantee approval. The lender may separately assess the property's title, valuation, approvals and transaction documents.
