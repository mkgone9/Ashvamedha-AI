Home Loan Affordability Calculator 2026 — How Much House Can You Afford?

The house you can buy and the house you can comfortably afford are not always the same. Affordability should account for the EMI, down payment, transaction costs, emergency fund, existing debt and future goals.

Build your affordability number

  1. Start with stable monthly income.
  2. Subtract existing EMIs and essential commitments.
  3. Set aside money for emergency savings and insurance.
  4. Reserve room for long-term investing.
  5. Test the proposed housing EMI.
  6. Add ownership costs such as maintenance and property taxes where applicable.
Key idea: don't use a rigid “salary × X” rule as your only answer. Household obligations differ too much for one ratio to work for everyone.

Example

If a household has ₹1.5 lakh monthly take-home income, the affordability question is not simply “What loan will a bank approve?” Existing EMIs, children, parents, insurance, investments and the desired lifestyle can change the sustainable EMI substantially.

Don't forget the cash requirement

A buyer needs more cash than the down payment alone. Budget for registration and stamp duty where applicable, interiors, moving expenses, brokerage where applicable, lender charges and a post-purchase emergency reserve.

Stress-test the house

Ask: if the interest rate increases, if one income stops temporarily, or if a large medical/family expense occurs, can the household still meet the EMI without selling long-term investments?
