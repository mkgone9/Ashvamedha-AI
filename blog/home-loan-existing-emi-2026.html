Can I Get a Home Loan With Existing EMIs in 2026?

Yes, having an existing car, personal or education loan does not automatically mean a home loan is impossible. But existing EMIs reduce the cash flow available for the proposed housing EMI and therefore can affect eligibility.

What the lender needs to understand

  • Total documented income
  • Existing EMI obligations
  • Remaining tenure on those loans
  • Credit history and repayment behaviour
  • New property and proposed loan amount
Example: two people earning ₹1 lakh per month can have very different housing capacity if one already pays ₹30,000 of EMIs and the other does not.

Should you close an existing loan first?

Sometimes reducing existing debt can improve monthly cash flow. But using all savings to close a loan can also weaken emergency liquidity. Compare the interest cost, remaining tenure, cash reserve and home purchase timeline.

Don't hide liabilities

Accurate disclosure matters. A home loan is a long-term commitment, so build the plan around the real household balance sheet rather than an optimistic application.
