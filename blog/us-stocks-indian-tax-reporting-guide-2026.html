US Stocks Tax Reporting in India 2026: ITR, Dividends & Foreign Assets

Investing overseas creates reporting responsibilities in India that go beyond calculating your stock profit.

Three areas investors should understand

  • Capital gains: sale of US shares can create taxable gains in India for a resident investor.
  • Dividends: US-source dividends can involve US withholding and Indian tax treatment.
  • Foreign assets: eligible taxpayers may have foreign-asset reporting requirements in the ITR.

Foreign tax credit

Where foreign tax has been paid, Indian tax-credit rules may allow relief subject to the applicable provisions, documentation and prescribed filing requirements. Keep the US broker's statements and tax documents rather than relying on a portfolio-app screenshot.

Records worth keeping

  • Broker trade confirmations and annual statements
  • Dividend statements
  • Dates, quantities and purchase prices
  • Remittance records
  • Foreign tax withheld documents
  • Exchange-rate methodology used for Indian tax computation
Important: The correct ITR schedules and foreign-asset disclosures depend on your facts and tax status. A tax professional should verify the return before filing.

Common mistakes

Ignoring small dividend receipts, keeping only INR portfolio values, forgetting foreign-asset reporting, or assuming that broker-reported US tax automatically completes Indian compliance can create avoidable problems.

Before filing your ITR

Prepare one clean year-end file containing your US holdings, transactions, dividends, withholding and remittances. This makes review much easier for your CA or tax adviser.
