Home Loan Prepayment vs SIP in 2026 — Where Should Your Extra Money Go?

This is not simply “debt versus investment.” Prepaying a loan produces a relatively certain reduction in future interest, while an equity SIP has uncertain market returns and a long investment horizon.

Prepay loanContinue SIP
Reduces outstanding debtBuilds financial assets
Interest saving depends on loan rateReturns are market-dependent
Can improve future cash flowMaintains investment habit/liquidity

Don't compare 8.5% loan with a guaranteed 12% investment return

Equity returns are not guaranteed. A projected return is not equivalent to a contractual saving. Taxes, volatility, time horizon and risk tolerance also matter.

Balanced approach: first protect emergency reserves and essential insurance, then consider splitting surplus between debt reduction and long-term investing.

Questions to ask

  • What is your home-loan rate?
  • How long is left?
  • How large is your emergency fund?
  • Are you investing enough for retirement?
  • How comfortable are you with market volatility?
