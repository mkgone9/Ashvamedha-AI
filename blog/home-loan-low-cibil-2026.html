Home Loan With Low CIBIL Score in 2026 — What Are Your Options?

A weaker credit history can make borrowing more difficult or expensive, but a score is only one part of a lender's assessment. Income, existing debt, repayment history and the property also matter.

Before applying, check the credit report

  • Look for incorrect accounts or overdue entries.
  • Check whether old loans are correctly shown as closed.
  • Avoid unnecessary multiple applications in a short period.
  • Pay current obligations on time.
Don't chase a magic number: different lenders and products can have different credit policies. A score alone does not guarantee approval.

Can a co-applicant help?

A co-applicant with a strong documented income and credit profile may affect the overall application, but it does not erase property or affordability issues. The lender's policy determines how the co-applicant is assessed.

If an application was rejected

Understand the stated reason before applying repeatedly elsewhere. Improve the relevant issue where possible, then reassess the application.
