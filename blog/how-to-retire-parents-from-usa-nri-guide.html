How to Retire Your Parents from the US: An NRI's Monthly Income Plan

✍️ Manoj Kumar📅 November 2026⏱️ 10 min read📍 Ashvamedha Finance, Hyderabad
📚 Part of: Retirement & Parents guide →

"I want Amma and Nanna to stop working" is something I hear from almost every NRI client in the US. You earn in dollars, the rupee goes far in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, and yet sending money every month feels fragile. A layoff, a visa issue or your own child's college fees can stop it. Here is how to retire your parents from the US with an income they own.

The short answer

  • Find the number: if your parents need ₹40,000 a month, you need ₹4.8 Lakh a year of reliable income.
  • Now: gift a lump sum; they invest it in SCSS (8.2%) and POMIS (7.4%) in their own names.
  • Later: fund a guaranteed income plan for 10 years that pays a fixed income for 30 years and replaces your monthly transfer.

Step 1: The retirement number

List what your parents' household really spends in a month: groceries, power, gas, phone, maid, medicines, travel, festivals and family functions. For most families in Hyderabad and tier-2 towns of the Telugu states this is ₹25,000–₹50,000 for two people.

Multiply by 12. If they need ₹40,000 a month, the target is ₹4.8 Lakh a year. Add a 5–6% yearly increase for inflation when you plan beyond 10 years.

Step 3: Income from day one

NRIs can't open the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) or Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS) themselves. But your parents can, with money you gift them. Gifts from a child to a parent are not taxed in India.

SCSS · 8.2%For age 60+. Up to ₹30 Lakh per person, so ₹60 Lakh for both parents. Interest paid every quarter. ₹30 Lakh pays ₹61,500 a quarter.
POMIS · 7.4%Up to ₹9 Lakh single / ₹15 Lakh joint. Pays every month. ₹15 Lakh joint pays ₹9,250 a month.
Senior citizen FDs · ~7–7.5%Large banks currently offer seniors around 7–7.55% on select tenures. Use for amounts above the SCSS/POMIS limits.
RBI Floating Rate Bond · 8.05%For Jul–Dec 2026. 7-year bond, interest every 6 months, rate resets with the NSC rate.

These are safe and start paying immediately. Their weakness: the rate is fixed only for 5–7 years. When they mature, your parents reinvest at whatever the rate is then, and interest is taxable in their hands.

US tax and reporting notes

If you are a US tax resident (H-1B, green card or citizen), keep these in mind and confirm with a cross-border CPA:

  • Gifts: up to $19,000 per parent per year (2026) needs no filing; above that, file Form 709. Tax applies only beyond the $15 million lifetime exemption.
  • Money transfers: use a US bank account or card. The 1% remittance tax from 2026 applies only to cash-funded transfers.
  • Policy in your own name: an Indian life insurance policy with a cash value may have to be reported (FBAR / Form 8938), premiums paid to a foreign insurer can attract a 1% US excise tax (Form 720), and the income may be taxable in the US even if it is tax-free in India.
  • Policy in your parent's name: generally simpler for you, since your parent is the resident Indian owner and you are just gifting the premium.

Five mistakes NRIs make

  1. No health insurance for parents, so one hospital bill wipes out the income plan.
  2. Buying a flat "for parents" that gives 2–3% rent and no liquidity.
  3. Keeping everything in one FD, so the income drops when rates fall.
  4. Putting parents' essential money in equity, so a market fall forces them to cut expenses.
  5. Relying only on monthly transfers, so a US layoff becomes your parents' crisis too.
🧠 Remember it: "Bridge now, secure later." SCSS/POMIS and your transfers are the bridge; a guaranteed income is the permanent road.

Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need to retire my parents in India from the US?

Work from their monthly expenses. For ₹40,000 a month you need about ₹4.8 Lakh a year. Around ₹60–65 Lakh in SCSS, POMIS and senior FDs can generate that today; alternatively, you can build a large part of it over 10 years through a guaranteed income plan.

Can an NRI buy a guaranteed income plan for parents?

Yes. You can buy it in your own name (higher income per rupee, but US reporting and tax rules apply) or pay the premium for a policy in your parent's name, within the insurer's entry-age and medical rules.

Can NRIs invest in SCSS?

No. NRIs are not eligible to open SCSS. Your resident parents (60+) can open it in their own names with money you gift them.

Is the income tax-free for my parents?

SCSS, POMIS and FD interest is taxable in their hands at slab rates, though seniors often pay little or no tax. Guaranteed income plan payouts are tax-free in India if the total yearly premium across such policies is ₹5 Lakh or less.

Should I send money monthly or invest a lump sum for my parents?

Ideally both: invest a lump sum (if you have it) in their names for immediate income, keep a monthly transfer as a bridge, and build a guaranteed income that takes over later.
