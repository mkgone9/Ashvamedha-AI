Tenure is one of the biggest home-loan decisions. A longer tenure lowers the scheduled EMI but generally increases total interest if the loan runs for the full term.

Why choose a longer tenure?

Some borrowers choose longer tenure to preserve monthly liquidity. That can be useful when the alternative is having an excessively tight cash flow. But the borrower should understand the interest cost if no prepayment occurs.

Why choose a shorter tenure?

A shorter scheduled tenure can reduce total interest, but it creates a larger mandatory monthly commitment. Don't choose it if doing so leaves no emergency or investment capacity.

Flexible strategy: some borrowers select a manageable tenure and later prepay when cash flow allows, subject to their loan terms and applicable rules.

What should decide the tenure?

  • Age and expected working period
  • Income stability
  • Existing debt
  • Family responsibilities
  • Emergency reserves
  • Long-term investing
