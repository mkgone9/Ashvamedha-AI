Family Office in India 2026 — What It Does, Who Needs One & Costs

As wealth grows, managing investments is only one part of the problem. Families may also need help coordinating taxation, estate planning, insurance, philanthropy, business interests, liquidity and multiple investment managers. That is where the family-office model comes in.

Simple definition: a family office is a professional structure created to coordinate and manage financial and, depending on the model, non-financial affairs of a wealthy family.

What does a family office actually do?

A family office can coordinate several areas rather than focusing on only one product.

  • Investment and portfolio oversight
  • Asset allocation across listed and alternative assets
  • Cash-flow and liquidity planning
  • Tax and estate-planning coordination with qualified professionals
  • Insurance and risk management
  • Reporting and consolidated portfolio tracking
  • Succession and inter-generational wealth planning
  • Philanthropy and family governance, where required

Single-family office vs multi-family office

ModelSimple explanation
Single-family officeDedicated structure serving one family, usually justified only at substantial scale.
Multi-family officeProfessional platform serving multiple families and sharing infrastructure and costs.
Advisory-led modelA family works with external specialists rather than building a full internal office.

Who may need a family office?

There is no single SEBI-defined net-worth number at which a family must create a family office. The decision is usually about complexity as much as wealth. A family with multiple businesses, properties, trusts, investments, family members and succession issues may have a stronger operational need than a family with the same net worth but a simple financial life.

What a family office should NOT become

  • A collection of product sales pitches.
  • A substitute for a qualified tax or legal professional.
  • A black box where family members cannot understand their own assets.
  • A reason to hold complicated investments without understanding liquidity and risk.

How much does a family office cost?

There is no universal price. Cost depends on whether the family builds an internal team, uses a multi-family office or combines specialist providers. Ask for the total cost of advice, portfolio management, fund expenses, custody, legal, tax and other professional services rather than looking only at one headline fee.

A practical family-office checklist

  1. Create a consolidated balance sheet.
  2. Separate operating-business risk from family wealth.
  3. Define liquidity requirements for 1, 3 and 10 years.
  4. Map insurance and major liabilities.
  5. Document investment policies and risk limits.
  6. Review succession and nomination arrangements.
  7. Use qualified tax and legal professionals for specialist matters.

Building a family wealth structure?

Start with a consolidated view of assets, liabilities, cash flow, investments and family goals before adding more products.
