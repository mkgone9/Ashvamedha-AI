What the table tells you

Moving from 20 to 30 years reduces the monthly EMI by roughly ₹4,945, but the additional interest over the full scheduled tenure is substantial. This is why tenure should be a financial decision, not merely an EMI decision.

Planning idea: if you choose a long tenure for flexibility, consider whether your cash flow allows future prepayments without sacrificing your emergency fund and other goals.
