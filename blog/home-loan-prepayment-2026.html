Home Loan Prepayment in 2026 — Should You Prepay Your Loan?

Prepayment can reduce future interest because you reduce outstanding principal earlier. But the decision should be balanced against liquidity and other financial goals.

What happens when you prepay?

A lump-sum payment reduces outstanding principal. Depending on the lender and your instruction, the resulting benefit may be reflected through a lower EMI, shorter tenure, or another permitted adjustment.

Prepaying can make sense when

  • You have a stable emergency fund.
  • High-cost debt is already under control.
  • Your essential insurance is covered.
  • You still have adequate long-term investments.
  • The loan rate and guaranteed interest saving make debt reduction attractive to you.
Don't empty your bank account: a debt-free property is not helpful if you have no liquidity for emergencies.

Reduce EMI or reduce tenure?

If your goal is to minimize total interest, reducing tenure generally keeps the repayment schedule shorter. If your goal is monthly cash-flow relief, reducing EMI may be more useful. The correct choice depends on your situation.

Prepayment charges

Applicable RBI rules and lender terms depend on the type of loan and borrower. For covered floating-rate loans to individual borrowers, RBI regulations restrict certain foreclosure/prepayment charges. Always check the current rule and your specific agreement.
