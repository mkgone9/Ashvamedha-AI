Fixed Income Investments in India: 2026 Beginner's Guide

✍️ Manoj Kumar📅 November 2026⏱️ 9 min read📍 Ashvamedha Finance, Hyderabad

Fixed income is the part of your money that is meant to be boring: it pays a known amount on known dates. But "fixed" hides an important question that most people never ask: fixed for how long? This guide explains the types of fixed income in India, how each is taxed, and why the lock-in period of the rate matters as much as the rate itself.

The short answer

  • Fixed income = investments that pay a set rate or amount: FDs, small savings, bonds, debt funds, guaranteed insurance plans and annuities.
  • Always ask "fixed for how long?" An FD fixes the rate for up to 10 years; a guaranteed income plan fixes the income for 30+ years.
  • Most fixed income is taxed at your slab. PPF, SSY and guaranteed plans (within ₹5 Lakh premium) are tax-free.

What is fixed income?

A fixed income investment is one where you lend money (to a bank, the government, a company or an insurer) and in return get interest or a payout on a schedule, plus your money back at the end. Unlike shares, the return is known in advance.

Its jobs in your portfolio: stability (it doesn't crash with the market), income (regular payouts) and certainty for goals (school fees in 3 years, retirement income from 60).

Types of fixed income in India

🏦 Bank & post office depositsFDs, RDs, Post Office Time Deposits (7.5% for 5 years). Simple, liquid with a penalty.
🇮🇳 Small savings schemesPPF 7.1%, NSC 7.7%, SCSS 8.2%, POMIS 7.4%, SSY 8.2% (Oct–Dec 2026). Government-backed; rates reviewed every quarter.
📜 Government bondsRBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds (8.05%), G-secs and T-bills (buyable via RBI Retail Direct).
🏢 Corporate bonds & NCDsHigher rates, but carry the company's credit risk. Check the rating.
📈 Debt mutual fundsPools of bonds. Liquid and diversified, but NAV moves with interest rates; returns not fixed.
🛡️ Guaranteed insurance plans & annuitiesFixed lump sum or yearly income written into the contract for 20–40 years, or for life.

Reinvestment risk, with numbers

Suppose you put ₹20 Lakh in a 5-year FD at 7.5%. You get ₹1.5 Lakh a year. Five years later, if FD rates are 5.5% (as they were in 2020–21), the same ₹20 Lakh gives only ₹1.1 Lakh a year. Your income drops by 27% and you did nothing wrong.

Interest rates in India have moved in big cycles: RBI cut rates sharply in 2020, raised them in 2022–23, and cut them again in 2025. Anyone living on deposit interest has seen their income move with each cycle. That is reinvestment risk, and it hits retirees hardest.

Key insight: If a goal is 20–30 years long (like retirement income), use at least some fixed income whose payout is locked for that whole period.

Laddering: the simple fix

Don't put everything in one deposit that matures on one date. Split it so something matures every year or two, and combine short locks with long locks:

You earn

Guaranteed income plan: income fixed for 30 years

If rates fall, only a small part renews at the lower rate. If rates rise, you get to reinvest some money at the higher rate.

How fixed income is taxed

FD, RD, SCSS, POMIS, NSC, RBI bonds, corporate bondsInterest taxed at your slab rate
Debt mutual funds (bought after 1 April 2023)Gains taxed at your slab rate
PPF, Sukanya SamriddhiTax-free
Guaranteed / endowment insurance plansTax-free if total yearly premium ≤ ₹5 Lakh (policies after 1 April 2023)
Annuity / pension incomeTaxed at slab rate

For someone in the 30% slab, a 7.5% FD gives about 5.2% after tax (more after cess), while a guaranteed plan within the ₹5 Lakh limit keeps its full ~6–6.5%. That difference is why tax-efficient fixed income matters. Details: the ₹5 Lakh rule.

Frequently asked questions

What are fixed income investments?

Investments that pay a predetermined interest rate or payout on a schedule and return your money at the end: bank and post office deposits, small savings schemes, government and corporate bonds, debt funds, guaranteed insurance plans and annuities.

Which fixed income investment gives the highest return in India now?

Among government-backed options, SCSS and Sukanya Samriddhi pay 8.2% and RBI Floating Rate Bonds 8.05% (late 2026). After tax, and for long periods, a guaranteed income plan within the ₹5 Lakh premium limit can compare well because it is tax-free and locked for decades.

Is fixed income better than equity?

Neither is better; they do different jobs. Equity grows wealth over long periods with ups and downs. Fixed income gives stability and predictable income. Most people need both.

What is reinvestment risk?

The risk that when your deposit or bond matures, interest rates are lower, so you must reinvest at a lower rate and your income falls.

Are debt mutual funds fixed income?

They invest in fixed-income securities, but their returns are not fixed: NAVs move with interest rates and credit events. For gains on units bought after 1 April 2023, tax is at your slab rate.
