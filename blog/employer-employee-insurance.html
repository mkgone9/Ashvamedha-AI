Employer-Employee Insurance — Benefits, Taxation & How It Works

✍️ Manoj Kumar📅 July 2025⏱️ 11 min read📍 Ashvamedha Finance, Hyderabad

Employer-employee insurance is one of the most tax-efficient ways for businesses to protect key employees while reducing their tax liability. Yet very few small business owners and entrepreneurs in India use it. Here's everything you need to know.

What is Employer-Employee Insurance?

In an employer-employee insurance scheme, a company (employer) buys a life insurance policy on the life of its employee. The employer pays the premium. The employee (or their family) is the beneficiary.

It is a legitimate group benefits structure recognised by IRDAI and the Income Tax Act.

How Employer-Employee Insurance Works

  1. Company selects eligible employees (all employees, or specific grades)
  2. Company buys group term life cover / individual policies on employees' lives
  3. Company pays premiums
  4. In case of employee's death — family receives the claim
  5. If employee leaves — policy can be assigned to the employee personally, or it lapses

Tax Benefits — For the Employer

This is the big attraction. Premium paid by the employer for employee insurance is treated as a business expense under Section 37(1) of the Income Tax Act — it is fully deductible from business income.

Example: A company with taxable profit of ₹50 lakh pays ₹5 lakh in employee insurance premiums. Taxable profit reduces to ₹45 lakh. Tax saving at 25% corporate rate = ₹1.25 lakh.

Tax Treatment — For the Employee

The premium paid by the employer is treated as a perquisite in the employee's hands — added to salary income and taxed at the employee's slab rate. However, if the employee uses Section 80C, the premium is deductible.

The sum assured received by the family on death is tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Who Can Take Employer-Employee Insurance?

  • ✅ Any registered company (Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership, Proprietorship)
  • ✅ For any employee — directors, managers, staff, even contractual employees in some cases
  • ✅ Even a sole proprietor can insure themselves under this structure with proper setup
  • ❌ Cannot be taken by a company for non-employees (suppliers, consultants without employment contract)

Benefits for Employees — Beyond the Insurance

  • Sense of financial security — family protected even if employee passes away unexpectedly
  • No medical examination in group policies typically (for standard cover amounts)
  • Premium paid by employer — zero out-of-pocket cost for employee
  • Can be converted to individual policy when employee leaves (portability)

How to Set It Up

  1. Decide on cover amount — typically 2–5x annual salary per employee
  2. Contact an insurance advisor with experience in group/corporate plans
  3. Submit employee list, salaries, and required cover amounts
  4. Insurer issues master policy to company, individual certificates to employees
  5. Premium paid by company annually / quarterly
