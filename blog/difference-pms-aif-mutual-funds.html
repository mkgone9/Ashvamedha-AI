PMS vs AIF vs Mutual Funds — Which Is Right for Your Wealth Level?

✍️ Manoj Kumar📅 July 2025⏱️ 13 min read📍 Ashvamedha Finance, Hyderabad

Mutual funds for the masses. PMS for the affluent. AIF for the ultra-wealthy. But the reality is more nuanced. This complete comparison helps you understand what each offers, what each costs, and which genuinely suits your financial situation.

The Three Tiers of Managed Investment in India

India's managed investment landscape has three distinct tiers regulated by SEBI, each designed for different wealth levels and sophistication:

Where Mutual Funds Win

  • Cost: Index funds at 0.1% expense ratio are the cheapest managed investment globally
  • Liquidity: Exit any time with no penalty (except ELSS lock-in)
  • Regulation: Strictest oversight — SEBI mandates daily NAV disclosure, third-party custody, audited performance
  • SIP discipline: Auto-debit SIPs make consistent investing effortless
  • Tax on equity MF: LTCG at 12.5% after just 12 months — efficient

Where PMS Wins

  • Personalisation: Portfolio can be customised — exclude specific sectors, add specific mandates
  • Concentrated bets: PMS can hold 15–25 stocks vs MFs 50–100 stocks — better alpha potential
  • Access to manager: Direct relationship with fund manager — not just a call center
  • Tax-loss harvesting control: You can request the manager to book specific losses for tax efficiency

Where AIF Wins

  • Access to exclusive deals: Pre-IPO unlisted shares, VC deals, private equity — unavailable in MF or PMS
  • Potential alpha: Unlisted investments can generate 3–5x in 5 years if selected well
  • Portfolio diversification: Decorrelated from public markets — alternative sources of return
  • Institutional quality due diligence: AIF managers invest full-time in researching deals you'd never find
