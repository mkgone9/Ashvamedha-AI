What happens when tenure increases?

A longer tenure normally reduces EMI but increases the number of months over which interest is paid. For example, a ₹50 lakh loan at 8.5% has an approximate EMI of ₹43,391 for 20 years and about ₹40,265 for 25 years. The lower EMI comes with a longer interest period.

Remember: never choose tenure only because the EMI looks comfortable. Compare total interest, liquidity and your other financial goals.

The EMI formula

EMI uses principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly instalments. You don't need to calculate it manually; the important decision is how the resulting EMI fits your household cash flow.

Before using an EMI calculator

  • Know the actual loan amount you need.
  • Keep down payment and purchase costs separate.
  • Include existing EMIs.
  • Test a higher interest rate.
  • Keep an emergency fund after purchase.
