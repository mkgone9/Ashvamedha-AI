Documents Required for Home Loan in India 2026

Documentation proves identity, income, repayment capacity and the legal status of the property. Exact requirements vary by lender and applicant profile, so use this as a preparation checklist rather than a universal list.

For salaried applicants

  • PAN and identity/address documents
  • Recent salary slips
  • Bank statements as requested by the lender
  • Employment or income details
  • Existing loan statements where applicable

For self-employed applicants

  • PAN and identity/address documents
  • Income-tax returns and computation as applicable
  • Bank statements
  • Financial statements/business income documents as requested
  • Business constitution/registration documents where applicable

Property documents

The lender may require sale agreements, title documents, approvals, encumbrance-related documents, plans and other papers depending on the property and transaction. A legal and technical evaluation may form part of the lender's process.

Practical tip: keep clean digital copies of income documents and bank statements. Missing or inconsistent information can slow processing.

Why documentation matters

A lender is assessing both the borrower and the collateral. Strong income documentation does not remove the need for acceptable property documentation, and a good property does not automatically overcome weak repayment capacity.

Before submitting

  1. Check names and dates match across documents.
  2. Keep explanations ready for large unusual bank transactions.
  3. Disclose existing liabilities accurately.
  4. Ask the lender for the exact checklist for your case.
