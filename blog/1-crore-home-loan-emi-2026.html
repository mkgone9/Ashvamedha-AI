What makes a ₹1 crore loan different?

The absolute EMI is large enough that even a modest change in rate or household income can materially affect cash flow. This makes stress testing especially important.

Before borrowing

  • Retain an emergency reserve after purchase.
  • Don't stop essential insurance.
  • Don't assume future bonuses will pay the EMI.
  • Consider retirement contributions.
  • Budget for property ownership costs.
Think in decades: a 20–30 year loan should be assessed against your full financial life, not just today's salary.
