Guaranteed Income: Build a Second Income That Never Stops

✍️ Manoj Kumar📅 November 2026⏱️ 9 min read📍 Ashvamedha Finance, Hyderabad
📚 Part of: Guaranteed Income guide →

Most families in India run on one income: a salary. When it stops, because of a layoff, illness or simply retirement, everything stops with it. A second income changes that. This guide explains how a guaranteed income plan builds a second income that arrives every year for 30 years, with the numbers, the tax rules and the trade-offs laid out honestly.

The short answer

  • A guaranteed income plan turns 10 years of savings into a fixed income for 30 years, plus your premiums back at the end.
  • Example: a 30-year-old paying ₹2 Lakh a year gets about ₹2,27,710 every year from age 43 to 72, then ₹20 Lakh back.
  • It is a safe, predictable second income, not the highest-return one. Pair it with SIPs for growth.

Why one income is a risk

A salary feels permanent until it isn't. IT layoffs, a company restructuring, a health break or a business slowdown can cut it overnight. Even if nothing goes wrong, it ends anyway at retirement. Meanwhile the EMIs, school fees and your parents' expenses keep coming.

A second income is money that comes in whether or not you go to work that month. Its job is simple: cover a part of your fixed expenses, so one shock doesn't break the household.

Five ways to build a second income

🏠 Rental incomeSteady, but needs a large down payment, EMIs, tenants and repairs. Yields in Hyderabad are often only 2–3% of the flat value.
🧑‍💻 Side business / freelancingCan be large, but needs your time every week and is not guaranteed.
📈 Dividends & SWP from fundsGrows with markets over the long run, but the amount moves with markets.
🏦 Interest (FD, SCSS, bonds)Safe, but the rate is fixed only for 1–7 years. When rates fall, your income falls.
🛡️ Guaranteed income planYou pay for a fixed period, then get a fixed amount every year for decades. Locked on day one.

Each has a place. The guaranteed income plan is the only one where the amount and the dates are fixed in a contract for 40+ years, with no tenant, no market and no reinvestment risk. That makes it a good base layer for a second income.

How a guaranteed income plan works

The structure most people choose is "Pay 10, Get 30":

You pay
10 years
a fixed amount every year
You wait
2 years
no payment, no income
You earn
30 years
fixed income every year
At the end
100%
of your premiums back
🧠 Remember it: Pay–Wait–Earn: 10 · 2 · 30. Pay for 10 years, wait 2, earn for 30, and get your money back at the end.

Example: Kiran, 30, a software engineer in Hyderabad, pays ₹2 Lakh a year from age 30 to 39.

  • From age 43 to 72, he gets about ₹2,27,710 every year: that's ₹68.3 Lakh in total.
  • At 72, he also gets ₹20 Lakh back (his total premiums).
  • Throughout the policy, his life is covered for about ₹21.5 Lakh, so his family is protected too.

He pays ₹20 Lakh and receives about ₹88 Lakh over the life of the plan. The amount does not depend on interest rates, the Sensex or his employer.

A simple way to read the table: each year's income is a little more than one year's premium, and it comes 30 times. That's why the plan works as a second salary rather than a one-time maturity.

The income ladder: build more than one stream

You don't have to do it all at once. Many of my clients build an income ladder: one plan now, another when their salary grows.

By 51, three streams together pay about ₹5 Lakh a year, around ₹42,000 a month, without your salary. Smaller steps also keep each year's total premium within the tax-free limit (next section).

Tax: when it is tax-free

For life insurance policies issued on or after 1 April 2023, the income and maturity are tax-free only if your total yearly premium across such traditional policies is ₹5 Lakh or less. Above that, the gain (what you receive minus what you paid) becomes taxable as income. This rule was in Section 10(10D) of the old Act and continues under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (Schedule II, read with section 11) from April 2026.

Rule of thumb: keep your combined yearly premium for guaranteed / endowment plans at or below ₹5 Lakh. If you want to invest more, split it with your spouse in their own name.

Within that limit, the return works out to roughly 6–6.5% a year, tax-free. For someone in the 30% slab, a taxable FD would need to pay around 9% to match it after tax. Read the detailed rules in are guaranteed income plans tax-free?

The honest trade-off: equity mutual funds have historically done better than 6–6.5% over long periods, but with no guarantee and with years of losses along the way. A guaranteed income plan gives up some upside for certainty. Most families do best with both: SIPs for growth, a guaranteed income for the base. Compare the two in guaranteed income plan vs SWP.

Also remember inflation: ₹2.27 Lakh in 13 years will buy less than it does today. That's why laddering, starting more plans as your income grows, works better than one big plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest way to build a second income in India?

A guaranteed income plan from a life insurer, the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (for 60+) and RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds are among the safest. Only the guaranteed income plan fixes the amount for 30 years; interest-based options reset when they mature.

How much should I invest to get ₹20,000 a month as a second income?

Roughly ₹2.1 Lakh a year for 10 years if you start around age 30, based on current illustrations. That gives about ₹2.39 Lakh a year (≈ ₹20,000 a month) for 30 years. Check your exact age on the calculator.

Is the income from a guaranteed income plan really guaranteed?

Yes. The yearly income and lump sum are written into the policy contract on day one by an IRDAI-regulated insurer. They do not change with interest rates or markets, as long as you pay all premiums.

Can I stop paying in between?

You can, but it is costly. The policy may become reduced paid-up or you may surrender it for less than you paid, especially in the early years. Choose an amount you can comfortably pay for all 10 years.

Is a second income from this plan taxable?

For policies issued after 1 April 2023, it is tax-free if your total yearly premium across such policies is ₹5 Lakh or less and the other conditions are met. Above ₹5 Lakh, the gain is taxable.
