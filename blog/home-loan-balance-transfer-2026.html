Home Loan Balance Transfer in 2026 — Is It Worth It?

A balance transfer moves an outstanding home loan from one lender to another, subject to the new lender's approval and terms. It can reduce interest cost in some cases, but the decision requires a complete cost comparison.

When it may be worth examining

  • Your outstanding principal is still substantial.
  • There is a meaningful rate difference.
  • A long enough tenure remains.
  • Transfer costs do not consume the potential saving.
Simple calculation: estimate interest under the existing loan, estimate interest under the proposed loan, then subtract transfer/processing/legal/other applicable costs.

Don't compare only the rate

Check the new benchmark/spread, reset terms, processing costs, legal/technical charges, insurance requirements where applicable and repayment conditions.

Also ask whether prepayment is the better route

If you have surplus cash, reducing the existing loan may achieve some of the same objective without moving lenders. Compare both paths.
