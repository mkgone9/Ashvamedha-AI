The part most videos skip

The ₹10.4 crore depends on earning 12% every year for 30 years while withdrawing. Move the slider to see how sensitive it is.

A bad year early in retirement hurts most: with a 20% fall in the first year and 12% after that, the money runs out at —. This is why a retirement plan needs a safety cushion and a yearly review.

Assumptions & what's not included
  • Returns of 14% (equity), 9% (hybrid) and 12% (after retirement) are assumptions, not guarantees. Real returns vary year to year and can be negative.
  • Figures are before tax. Withdrawals from mutual funds attract capital gains tax, which lowers the income or the amount left.
  • No fund charges beyond what the assumed returns already reflect; no other income or expenses.
  • This is one general example, the same for everyone. It is not personal advice and not a recommendation to buy any fund.
