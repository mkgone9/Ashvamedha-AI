What if returns are lower?

A bad year early in retirement hurts most: with a 20% fall in the first year and your assumed return after that, —. This is why a retirement plan needs a safety cushion and a yearly review.

Assumptions & what's not included
  • All returns are assumptions, not guarantees. Real returns vary year to year and can be negative.
  • Figures are before tax. Withdrawals from mutual funds attract capital gains tax, which lowers the income or the amount left.
  • Money is invested once today; income is taken at the end of each month, starting the month after retirement. No other income, expenses or fund charges beyond what the returns already reflect.
  • Inflation for "today's money" is taken as 6% a year.
  • This is a general calculator for education. It is not personal advice and not a recommendation to buy any fund.
